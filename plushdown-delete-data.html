Request Data Deletion

Plushdown by Adawworks. Last updated: October 22, 2026

You can ask us to delete the data that Plushdown stored on our server about you. Plushdown does not use email sign-up or passwords, so we find your data by your player name.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to Adawworks.studio@gmail.com with the subject Plushdown data deletion request.
  2. In the email, write the player name you used in Ranked Match.
  3. We will reply to confirm, and delete your data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your ranked player record: anonymous account ID, player name, rank points and season data.
  • Your run results.
  • The builds you uploaded for PvP fights (items, skills, level, health and similar game values).

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you is kept after deletion. Copies in server backups are removed as the backups expire, within 30 days of the deletion.

Data on your device

Your progress, settings and tutorial progress are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes them. We cannot see or delete this data for you.
