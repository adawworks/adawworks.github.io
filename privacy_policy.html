Plushdown Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Plushdown ("the game") is made by Adawworks ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and what happens to it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Summary

  • The game has no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.
  • Normal Game works completely on your device. Ranked Match connects to our server to store your rank and to let other players face a copy of your build.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only: your player name, settings (such as volume), the run in progress, and tutorial progress. This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Information sent to our server (Ranked Match only)

When you play Ranked Match, the game connects to our server (hosted by Supabase) and sends:

  • A random anonymous account ID created by the game automatically. You do not sign up with an email or password.
  • The player name you chose in the game (please do not use your real name or personal information).
  • Your rank points, season and run results.
  • The build you used in each PvP fight of a ranked run: your items, skills, level, health and similar game values.

Other players may fight a copy of your build as an opponent, and the player name you chose and your rank may be shown to them and on leaderboards. Normal Game runs are not uploaded.

How we use the information

Only to run the game: to keep your rank, to find fair opponents, to show leaderboards and to keep the game working and fair. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent information to anyone. The server provider (Supabase) stores the data for us as a service provider. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Ranked data is kept while the game and its servers are running, and may be removed or reduced over time (for example, older builds may be deleted). Data that is not tied to your name cannot be traced back to you.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your ranked data (player record and uploaded builds) by emailing us from the address below and telling us your player name. Uninstalling the game removes everything saved on your device.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The game does not ask for personal information. If you believe a child has put personal information in the player name, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

Data is sent to the server over an encrypted connection. No online service can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Changes

If the game changes (for example, adding sign-in with Google Play, ads or purchases), we will update this page and the date above before the change goes live.
